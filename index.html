Every bill has exactly one policy area

Analysts at the Congressional Research Service read each bill and file it under one of 32 policy areas. Hopper uses 8 of them. Every card says what its bill would do. The crate only blushes its bin's colour near the ground, so reading is the faster way.

You don't choose the bill. You choose the bin.

The Capitol launches the next bill every 5 seconds. Four steps, then the details.

  1. Read the card

    Each bill's card shows its title and one plain line about what it would do. The same line sits in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

  2. Drag it to a bin

    Drop the crate on the bin for its policy area. Green means right. Red is a strike, and the right bin glows gold so you learn it.

  3. Beat the colour

    Near the ground the crate blushes its bin's colour. Reading is faster, and the end card counts how often you sorted before the hint.

  4. Open your docket

    25 right finishes the dome; 3 strikes in one bin is gridlock. Then open every bill you handled to see what it would do and how far it has gotten.
